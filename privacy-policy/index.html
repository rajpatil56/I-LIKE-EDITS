Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026

Your images never leave your device

i like edits 👍 is built differently from most “online” image tools: every compress, resize, crop, convert, watermark and editing operation runs locally in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and WebAssembly. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers, so there is nothing for us to store, see or delete.

The one exception: Remove Background

The background-removal tool downloads a small AI model to your browser the first time you use it (so it can run the segmentation locally). That download is just the model’s weights — your photo is still processed entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Local, temporary data

Files you process are handled securely in your browser and never uploaded to our servers. Anything temporarily cached locally clears automatically within 2 hours, and you can clear it yourself any time.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics to understand which tools are useful. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us any time at privacy@pixelforge.app.
