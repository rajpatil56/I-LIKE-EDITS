Security & Privacy by Design

i like edits 👍 processes images client-side, in your browser. That one architectural choice removes most of the usual risks of an “upload your photo to our server” tool.

Files auto-deleted within 2 hours

Files you process are handled securely in your browser and never uploaded to our servers. Anything temporarily cached locally clears automatically within 2 hours, and you can clear it yourself any time.

Secure HTTPS connection

The app itself is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and because processing happens on your device, your images never travel over the network at all.

100% in-browser processing

Every tool on this site runs locally in your browser using the Canvas and WebAssembly APIs. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Found a security issue?

Please report it responsibly to security@pixelforge.app.
